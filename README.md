# David Tuisaula

Full stack developer in New Zealand.

I came to software the long way round. I spent years in residential and commercial real estate, worked at an internet provider, and then took a few years out as a stay-at-home dad. Somewhere in there I built a system at Ray White to keep the agent database in order, and eventually decided to learn how to do that kind of thing properly.

I've just finished a Level 5 diploma in advanced full stack development at Mission Ready. Most of what's on this profile came out of that.

## Things I've built

- **[Tina](https://github.com/King5ula/Turners-Insurance-Chatbot)** is an insurance chatbot that asks about your car and recommends a policy. React, Express, the Claude API, and Docker.
- **[Z Energy station finder](https://github.com/King5ula/Z-energy-website-app-revamp-project)** was a team project. I built the Nearby Stations part: the station data in MongoDB, the map, the station cards and the fuel price comparison.
- **[Vehicle recognition for Turners](https://github.com/King5ula/Turners-Cars-AWS-Rekognition-ML)** takes a photo of a car and tells you the body type and make, using a model I trained on AWS Rekognition.
- **[Insurance quote APIs](https://github.com/King5ula/L5-mission-2)** are two small Express APIs written test first with Jest and Supertest.
- **My portfolio** ([frontend](https://github.com/King5ula/my-portfolio-frontend), [backend](https://github.com/King5ula/my-portfolio-backend)) is Next.js and TypeScript on the front and Python with FastAPI on the back. It has a chatbot that answers questions as me, which is either a good idea or a terrible one.

I'm also building a field service app for a generator company in Auckland. That one isn't public yet.
